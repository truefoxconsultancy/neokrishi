The Guarantee, Explained

Why We Buy Back Every Kilo — The Science Behind the Guarantee

This isn't a subsidy. It's a supply chain — and you're the first link in it.

Your raw vermicompost and vermiwash don't stop at your gate. They move into our lab pipeline, where they're enriched with targeted bio-nutrient cultures — Azotobacter for nitrogen fixation, PSB (phosphate-solubilizing bacteria) for nutrient uptake, and Jeevamrutam for microbial activation. What comes out is a retail-ready product line sold at commercial-agriculture and urban-gardening margins: Neo Gold, our flagship enriched bio-fertilizer, and Neo Vermiboost, our concentrated liquid bio-input.

That's the entire reason the buyback works. We're not buying your output as a favor — we're buying raw material for a product line with real retail demand. Your production feeds a business that was already going to exist. The guarantee is structural, not promotional.
